The Atelier

Built like a haute horlogerie house.

Every CHRONO S begins as raw gold and steel in the AKCLNT atelier. Cases are milled, dials lacquered in seven coats, and each Calibre AK-72 is assembled by a single watchmaker — then tested for fifteen days before it ever meets a wrist.

01

Hand-finishing

Bridges bevelled and polished by hand; screws heat-blued one at a time. Details you'll need a loupe to see — finished anyway.

02

Single-watchmaker assembly

One watchmaker builds each movement from 217 parts to finished calibre, and signs the mainplate beneath the rotor.

03

15-day testing

Regulated in five positions across fifteen days. Only movements holding −2/+4 seconds per day leave the atelier.
